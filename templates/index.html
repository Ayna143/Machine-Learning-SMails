                How It Works
                
What Is Email Spam?

                
Spam emails are unsolicited messages sent in bulk, often containing phishing links, malware, or deceptive offers. They clutter your inbox and can lead to identity theft or financial loss if you engage with them.

            

            

                Machine Learning
                
How SpamSense Detects Spam

                
SpamSense uses three powerful ML models — Random Forest, Naive Bayes, and XGBoost — trained on thousands of real emails. We extract features like suspicious keywords, URL counts, sender patterns, and more to classify each message.

            

            

                Tips & Safety
                
How to Protect Yourself from Spam

                
Never click suspicious links. Check sender addresses carefully — scammers use look-alike domains. Enable two-factor authentication. Use tools like SpamSense to verify messages before acting on them.

            

            

                NLP
                
Understanding TF-IDF Vectorization

                
TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency) converts email text into numerical features. It highlights words that are important in a specific email but rare across all emails — a powerful signal for spam detection.

            

            

                Security
                
Phishing vs. Spam: Know the Difference

                
Spam is annoying junk mail. Phishing is a targeted attack that impersonates trusted entities to steal your credentials. Both are dangerous, but phishing emails are crafted to look legitimate and urgent.

            

            

                Feature Engineering
                
What Features Does SpamSense Analyze?

                
We analyze 16+ features: suspicious keywords, URLs, special characters, uppercase ratio, HTML presence, sender domain reputation, device type, exclamation marks, dollar signs, digit ratios, and more.
